## Dimension-by-dimension comparison

| Dimension | Anant Panchang | Today | Citizen Calculator Plus |
|-----------|----------------|-------|-------------------------|
| **Title keyword density** | Strong — brand + Hindu Calendar (30/30 chars) | **Weak** — single word “Today” | **Moderate** — “Plus” without GST |
| **Short description** | Strong — Tithi, Vrat, Muhurat, Festivals (80/80) | Good keywords; optional tighten | **Misordered** — unit/currency before GST |
| **Long description** | Feature blocks + festival keywords | Strong (UPSC, filters, games) | Strong (GST, themes, games, disclaimer) |
| **Screenshot story** | Should lead Timeline + Muhurat | Should lead observances + history + filters | Should lead GST + history tape |
| **Social proof** | Healthy | **Conversion risk** (3.2★) | **Conversion risk** (few reviews) |
| **Change required?** | No copy rewrite | **Yes — title** | **Yes — title + short** |
